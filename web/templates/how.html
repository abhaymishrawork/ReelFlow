How it works

You talk. We turn it into a reel people finish.

No editing app, no timeline, no transcript to fix. Here is exactly what happens to your video.

01

You upload a raw video

Record on your phone, talking to camera. Mistakes, restarts and long pauses are fine. Pick a style, add details only if you want to.

02

We understand your message

We transcribe your video and work out who it is for, what problem you are solving and what viewers should do next. This decides every visual choice.

03

We cut it tight

The best take of every line is kept. Retakes, filler and dead air go, so the reel moves fast without sounding chopped.

04

We design the visuals

Motion graphics explain your points, real logos and website screens appear when you mention a tool, and B-roll matches your audience.

05

We finish the sound and picture

Captions in your chosen style, headline moments, music under your voice, sound design and a colour grade.

06

You download and post

Your order page turns to Ready with a 1080×1920 download. Want a change? Ask for a revision.

Videos that work best

Great fit

  • Advice, tips and lessons
  • Explaining your product or service
  • Stories and case studies
  • One person talking to camera

Not a fit (yet)

  • Vlogs with no talking
  • Long podcasts over 5 minutes
  • Music videos
  • Videos you don't have the rights to
