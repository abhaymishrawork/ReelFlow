Questions

What kind of video should I upload?

A talking-to-camera video: advice, a lesson, a product explanation or a story. Vertical or horizontal both work.

Do I need to write a brief?

No. We work out the audience and message from what you say. Add context only if you want something specific.

How long does it take?

Most reels are delivered within 24 hours. Your order page updates automatically.

What happens to my video?

It is used only to edit your reel and is deleted 30 days after delivery.
