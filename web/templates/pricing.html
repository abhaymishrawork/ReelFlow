Pricing questions

What counts as one reel?

One raw video up to 5 minutes, edited into one vertical reel.

What is a revision?

A round of changes after delivery, like swapping a B-roll clip, changing a headline or trimming a section.

What makes Custom different?

A human — the person behind ReelFlow — personally reviews and finishes your reel, and keeps correcting it with you until it's right, at no extra charge.

Can I move between tiers later?

Yes. Order Basic for one reel and Creator for the next — there's no lock-in or subscription to cancel.
